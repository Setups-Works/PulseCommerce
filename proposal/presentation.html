Messaging · automation

Campaigns that run themselves

A campaign is one message, now. A flow is a sequence that runs over days, so a customer receives the right message at the right point rather than whenever somebody remembered to send one.

A worked example

A customer goes quiet past their own usual reorder gap.

Day 0 — a reminder naming the product they actually buy, with its photo and a link.

Day 3 — a 10% code, created in WooCommerce, restricted to that product, expiring in a fortnight.

Day 10 — a last note before the code lapses.

They order on day 4. Steps two and three never send.

How it behaves

Customers join as they qualify. The audience is re-checked on every run, so whoever lapses next month enters next month. A flow keeps working rather than sending once to whoever matched on the day.

Nobody enters twice, for the life of the flow.

They leave the moment it works. A customer who buys stops receiving the rest. A win-back that keeps nagging someone who already came back is worse than not running at all.

Why the timing holds

Each wait is measured from when the previous message actually sent, not from when the customer joined — so a delayed run never bunches two messages together.

A run that is missed delays a step. It cannot lose one, and it cannot send one twice.

Before a customer sees it

A flow can be pointed at one test number, so a whole sequence is checked on your own phone first. Flows are created as drafts; starting one is a separate, deliberate act.

The menu

A customer sends an agreed word — “hi” — and receives a numbered menu straight away:

1  Order  →  shop link and order tracking
2  Contact us  →  how to reach a person
3  Feedback  →  an invitation to just type it

Menus nest as deeply as needed. Each conversation remembers where it is and forgets after fifteen minutes, so a stray number the next day is never read as a menu choice.

It never intercepts a real question

Only the exact trigger word starts the menu. Anything else is left for a person to answer, so a customer asking a genuine question reaches your team rather than a robot.

It runs on the gateway itself, which is why it answers whether or not anything else is running.

What the figures mean

Measurement window

All store figures were computed from naturesjoystore.com covering 1 August 2025 to 31 July 2026, across 20,444 orders retrieved through the WooCommerce REST API. Figures change as the store trades and should be re-run before being relied upon.

How revenue is counted

Net revenue is order total less tax, shipping and refunds, counting only orders in completed, processing or on-hold status. Cancelled, failed and pending orders are excluded from revenue but included in cancellation rates. This may differ from figures shown elsewhere in WooCommerce, which counts differently.

How customers are counted

Guest checkouts carry no WooCommerce customer ID, so buyers are identified by billing email address. One person ordering under two email addresses counts as two customers. Customer counts and repeat rates are affected accordingly.

Projections and illustrations

Win-back revenue, campaign returns, break-even points and forecasts are illustrations based on stated assumptions. They are not forecasts, guarantees, warranties or commitments of any kind. Actual results will differ, potentially materially.

Statistical limits

Predicted lifetime value and churn risk are estimates derived from observed purchase behaviour. Forecasts use a least-squares trend with day-of-week seasonality and know nothing of planned promotions, stock availability, price changes or seasonality outside the period analysed.

Third-party pricing

WhatsApp Cloud API rates are set by Meta, vary by country and message category, and change without notice. The rates shown are illustrative ranges, not quotations. Confirm current pricing with Meta before making a decision on that basis.

Costs not included

Server hosting for the WhatsApp gateway, estimated at approximately ₹500 per month, is billed by your hosting provider and is not included in the quoted price. A dedicated phone number for WhatsApp sending is also not included. Domain, SSL and WooCommerce hosting remain your existing arrangements.

Inventory figures

Days of cover and revenue at risk are calculated from observed sales velocity and current stock levels where WooCommerce tracks them. Products without stock management enabled cannot be planned for.
